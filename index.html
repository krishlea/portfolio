Our Story

Born in a Kitchen,
Built for Every Home

Krishlea started when our founder Priya couldn't find a healthy snack her kids would actually enjoy. Everything on the shelf was either boring or packed with junk. So she started making her own — roasting makhana with spices from her mother's kitchen in Bihar.

Three years, 12,000 families, and three flavours later — we still make every batch the same way. Small. Careful. With love.

🌾
Sourced from Bihar's Best Farms We work directly with lotus farmers in Darbhanga — fair prices, fresh produce, every time.
🧪
Lab Tested, FSSAI Approved Every batch is tested before it leaves our facility. What's on the label is what's inside.
♻️
Minimal Packaging, Maximum Care Recyclable pouches, no plastic waste. Because the planet is part of the family too.
